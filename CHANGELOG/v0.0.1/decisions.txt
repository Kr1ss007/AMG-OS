AMGOS v0.0.1 Cycle Decisions
================================================================================
1. Internal IPC Naming:
   Designated as EventBus / e-bus for custom Unix socket pub/sub.
   External D-Bus bridge designated as EventOutsiderBus / eo-bus to maintain strict
   isolation and prevent third-party apps from reaching internal pub/sub directly.

2. Build Automation:
   Adopted just and root Justfile over makefile per project lead directive.

3. Two-Process Hard Boundary:
   Process 1 owns hardware, credentials, power, and AVM.
   Process 2 owns Wayland compositor and rendered UI.
   Process 2 never holds network credentials or raw filesystem write access.

4. Filer Built First:
   Building Filer first forces the IPC message contract between Process 1 and Process 2
   to be fully defined and verified before dependent services are added.
================================================================================
